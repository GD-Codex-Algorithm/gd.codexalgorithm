About this project

Who am I

I am specialized in game design and digital products. Passionate about combining the world of research and digital development to create new processes and enhancing experiences.

Why

In video game development, it's getting tougher to find and research algorithms. The term "artificial intelligence" is overused and makes research of algorithms and automation techniques time-consuming. Often all due to the prevailing presence of machine learning algorithms.

What

The idea is to create a digital library that gathers AI applications, systems, and tools used in the game development community. This would be a starting point for game developers to understand the complexities of AI systems and their potential use in games.

How

Building an accessible space showcasing examples and systems will make learning quick and easy. Users can discover points of interest and tactics used in other games. For instance, you could compare how enemies are made autonomous in Pac-Man versus how they are controlled in games like Call of Duty.

For Whom

Game designers and the game industry will gain from a structured database of information, documenting the pros and cons of systems and identifying areas that need more research.

Impact

Codex Algorithm evolves with AI and its impact on game development. The virtual library builds a community and resource archive to track AI advancements and aid design decisions. Often, the best solution isn’t the most elaborate but the one that looks the smartest in the simplest way.
